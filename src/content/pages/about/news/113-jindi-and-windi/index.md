## jINDI and wINDI are now available for download.

jINDI is still under heavy development but a "developer preview" of common modules, Telescope and CCD GUI is ready for testing. This crossplatform INDI client is now working with native INDI 0.95 drivers as well as with wINDI ASCOM wrappers. Visit [project page](http://www.cloudmakers.eu/jindi) to learn more.

Development of wINDI is finished and it is available as pre-release version. We are collecting feedback and fixing bugs found. Subproject now has its own [project page](http://www.cloudmakers.eu/windi).
