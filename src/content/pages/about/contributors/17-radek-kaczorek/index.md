Radek is a developer of drivers for Raspberry Pi, such as GPSD, Astroberry DIY (focuser, relays, gps), Astroberry PiFace (focuser, relays) , Astroberry AMH (Adafruit Motor HAT), Quantum Filter Wheel.

He's a developer and lead maintainer of [**Astroberry OS**](https://astroberry.io) (formerly known as Astroberry Server) which is  an operating system designed for controlling astronomy equipment supported by INDI.

Besides astronomy he enjoys software, hardware and mind hacking.
