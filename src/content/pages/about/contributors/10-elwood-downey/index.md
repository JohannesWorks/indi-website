Elwood was best known for his popular ephemeris application Xephem. Elwood was the author of the INDI Wire Protocol and developed the bulk of the core INDI library code, including the INDI server, tools, event loop, and lilxml.

We are saddened by the passing of Elwood C. Downey in January 2026.
